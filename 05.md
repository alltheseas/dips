"The simplest community spec"

### Problem Statement
Nostr today is a bitcoin meetup. Therefore bitcoiners by default find their people. However, for *non*-bitcoiners it is not trivial to accomplish the job-to-be-done of "finding my people". 

This DIP is a simple implementation of community spec/namespace that allows for Damus, and nostr users to voluntarily and freely associate with people who have similar interests. 

It is hypothesized that if new to nostr people are joining with or without invite to a nostr community, the ability to easily find, and join a community may improve interest in and retention of new users on nostr apps.
